How Dewlo works

1

Name the goal

Where do you want to be in a year? Pick an area or write your own.

2

Start small

Dewlo suggests one small habit to begin with, so it's easy to start.

3

Check in daily

Tap done, or be honest with "not today." Every honest check-in counts.

4

Review weekly

A quick weekly check-in looks at how it went and plans the next step.

Start with what you want

The first thing Dewlo asks is simple: where do you want to be in a year? Tap an area: Health, Happiness, Money, Career, or Other.

  • Health
  • Happiness
  • Money

Tap an area for ready-made goals and starter habits, or write your own goal by hand. Tap Other and type what you have in mind, and Dewlo suggests a clear goal you can keep, edit, or try again.

Dewlo asks before anything is shared with an outside service to help write your plan. Prefer to plan on your own? You can do that too.

Meet Dewlo

Dewlo is a little frog who grows as your habits stick: from an egg on the day you set your goal, to a tadpole, a froglet, and finally a full frog. Along the way you can unlock outfits for him, like a sweatband, a chef hat or a party hat.

Welcome to anyone with a dream

The first screen offers five areas: Health, Happiness, Money, Career, and Other. Tap one for ready-made goals, or tap Other and write your own, and Dewlo turns it into a clear suggested goal.
