Every dream starts small.

Reach your big goal, one small habit at a time.

Dewlo is a friendly goal and habit coach for iPhone. Everyone has a dream. Write yours down as a one-year goal, and Dewlo helps you get there with tiny daily habits and a short weekly check-in.

Coming to iPhone in 2027

How Dewlo works

1

Pick your goal

Choose an area or write your own goal. Dewlo helps turn it into a clear goal for the year.

2

Start small

Dewlo suggests one small habit that fits your real day, so it's easy to begin.

3

Check in daily

Tap done, or be honest with "not today." Every honest check-in counts.

4

Review weekly

A quick weekly check-in looks at how it went and plans the next step.

Pick an area to grow in

Start with the part of life that matters most to you right now, or write your own goal.

  • Health
  • Happiness
  • Money
  • Career
  • Other: write your own

Meet Dewlo

Dewlo is a little frog who grows as your habits stick: from an egg on the day you set your goal, to a tadpole, a froglet, and finally a full frog. Along the way you can unlock outfits for him, like a sweatband, a chef hat or a party hat.

About us

Dewlo is made by Houfu LLC, a small app company based in Florida. Houfu builds simple, friendly apps that help people make steady progress on what matters to them. Dewlo is our first app, and we're building it in the open. Follow along on TikTok and Instagram at @dewloapp.
