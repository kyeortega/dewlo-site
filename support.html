Support

Need help or have a question? Email support@dewlo.app and we'll reply within 2 business days. For anything else, write to hello@dewlo.app.

When does Dewlo come out?

Dewlo is coming to iPhone in 2027. We're running a small private test first.

Who can use Dewlo?

Dewlo is for adults 18 and over.

Is Dewlo therapy or medical advice?

No. Dewlo offers goal planning and habit support. It isn't therapy or medical care. If you're in crisis in the U.S., call or text 988.
